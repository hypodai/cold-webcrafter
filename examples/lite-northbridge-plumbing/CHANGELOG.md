## [Unreleased]

### Added
- A modern three-page preview of the Northbridge Plumbing & Heating website: Home, Services and Contact, in the business's own blues, with its real wording, numbers and customer quotes.
- Tap-to-call phone buttons on every page, a layout that works on phones, and an enquiry form with name, phone/email and message (it checks the entries and shows a confirmation).
- Page titles, search descriptions, social-sharing tags, local-business information for search engines, a sitemap and a robots file.
- Gentle scroll animations that respect the "reduce motion" setting.
- Redirects from the old addresses (for example `/about.html`) to the nearest of the three new pages.

### Changed
- The out-of-date 2016 footer is now the current year; the placeholder "Welcome to our website!" headline became a proper headline built from the business's own description.
- Images are resized and converted to a lighter format automatically.

### Fixed
- Removed the leftover placeholder news item ("Small Title" / lorem ipsum).

### Removed
